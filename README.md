## 📖 About Traumakorp

**Traumakorp** is an online bookstore created for readers who want an easy and organized way to explore books from different genres.

Our goal is to provide a clean, modern, and user-friendly shopping experience where customers can quickly discover books, browse categories, view prices, and find their next favorite read.

Traumakorp is designed for readers of all ages.
